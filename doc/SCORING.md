# Scoring Method

The transparent screening score combines:
- 25% fragility pressure (`100 - condition_score`)
- 22% historical value
- 14% annual demand
- 12% language rarity
- 10% uniqueness
- 10% digitization gap
- small adjustable contributions from digitization effort and accessibility gap

Classification:
- 0–24.99 Low
- 25–49.99 Moderate
- 50–74.99 High
- 75–100 Critical

The score is a prioritization aid and should be reviewed by qualified archival professionals.
